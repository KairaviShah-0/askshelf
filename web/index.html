FAQ

Questions we get asked

What data do I need?

One row per order line with a date and an amount is enough to start. Add cost, due and delivered dates, stock on hand and a penalty column to unlock margins, deliveries, penalties and stock cover. Each page tells you exactly which columns it still needs.

How does pricing work?

A flat monthly price per plan covers a set number of people, data sources and answers. Dashboards, alerts and exports are unlimited. If you need more answers than your plan includes, extra answers cost $20 per 1,000. Use the calculator above to see the cheapest plan for your team.

How is this different from a general chatbot?

A general chatbot cannot see your data and tends to guess at arithmetic. AskShelf routes each question to a vetted analysis that runs on your data, then explains the result. Numbers are repeatable and traceable.

Can it see customer or employee details?

No. Customer IDs, enquiry text, employee IDs and individual pay are removed when data is loaded. Requests for them are politely refused.

Can the forecasts be trusted?

Forecasts are back-tested and shown as ranges, not single numbers. Where a model has no real predictive power, AskShelf says so instead of faking confidence.
