Trust and security

Powerful answers. Strict boundaries.

Sensitive data is removed before analysis, every action is logged, and the AI never does your arithmetic.

Role-based access

Purchasing, warehouse, sales and finance each see only the analyses they are cleared for. Out-of-role requests are refused before any data is read.

Privacy by design

Customer and employee identifiers and individual pay are dropped when data is loaded. Output is scanned and redacted.

Tamper-evident audit trail

Every question is logged with its outcome and cost in a hash-chained record. Edit one line and the check fails.

Numbers you can trace

The language model never writes code or sees raw rows. It picks a vetted analysis, so every figure is repeatable.

A second pair of eyes

A critic pass checks each draft for leaked details, wrong numbers and claims the data cannot support.

Encrypted in transit

All traffic runs over TLS when deployed behind HTTPS, including calls to the language model.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

Is it built for wholesalers and distributors?

Yes. The questions it answers are the ones distribution teams ask every day: which purchase orders will be late, whether stock covers open orders, what is due this week and what penalties it could trigger. The demo runs on a simulated furniture business with orders, stock and delivery records; the same engine connects to your own order and stock exports.

How does pricing work?

A flat monthly price per plan covers a set number of people, data sources and answers. Dashboards, alerts and exports are unlimited. If you need more answers than your plan includes, extra answers cost $20 per 1,000. Use the calculator above to see the cheapest plan for your team.

How is this different from a general chatbot?

A general chatbot cannot see your data and tends to guess at arithmetic. AskShelf routes each question to a vetted analysis that runs on your data, then explains the result. Numbers are repeatable and traceable.

Can it see customer or employee details?

No. Customer IDs, enquiry text, employee IDs and individual pay are removed when data is loaded. Requests for them are politely refused.

Can the forecasts be trusted?

Forecasts are back-tested and shown as ranges, not single numbers. Where a model has no real predictive power, AskShelf says so instead of faking confidence.
